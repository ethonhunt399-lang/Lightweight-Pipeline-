SLBH Revit Bridge v0.3.4

Scope
- Circular element source optimization fix.
- Keeps v0.3.3 overall display proxy features.

Revit exporter
- Improved round structural member parameterization.
- Line-based round structural columns, framing and braces continue to export as straight_round_curve.
- Added conservative support for common point-based vertical round steel columns:
  - no active section box
  - readable diameter or safe bounding-box diameter estimate
  - bounding-box horizontal extents match the detected diameter
  - height is clearly larger than diameter
- Unreliable, cut, non-round, short, or complex members still fall back to Revit mesh export.
- Added bounding-box based diameter fallback for round members with missing diameter parameters.

Blender importer
- Reduced circular reconstruction segment counts:
  - Fast: 8 sides for <= 300 mm, 12 sides above 300 mm
  - Standard: 12 sides for <= 300 mm, 16 sides above 300 mm
  - Fine: 16 sides for <= 300 mm, 24 sides above 300 mm
- This reduces polygon count for straight round pipes, round ducts and round structural columns generated from Revit parametric records.

Notes
- The exporter still prioritizes model correctness. If a round-looking family cannot be proven to be a simple straight round member, it remains a mesh.
- Real Revit 2019/2020 and Blender 4.5 LTS validation is required on production models.
